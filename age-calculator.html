How the calculation works

Age in whole years is today's date minus the birth year, adjusted down by one if this year's birthday hasn't happened yet. Months and days are found the same way, working through the remaining months since the last birthday. Total days lived is simply the number of calendar days between the birth date and today, which is why it can look larger than expected — a 30-year-old has lived roughly eleven thousand days.

What does an age calculator calculate?

An age calculator works out a person's age from a date of birth to a selected date. It can show the result in years, months and days, which is more useful than simply subtracting birth years when the birthday has not occurred yet in the current year.

For practical planning, the exact result depends on the dates entered. If you are checking an official age requirement, always use the date and calculation method specified by the relevant organization.

Common uses

People use age calculations for birthdays, school or exam eligibility, application forms, retirement planning and other situations where an exact age matters.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator know my birthday? No. You enter the date yourself and the calculation is performed from the supplied values.

Can I calculate age on a future date? Yes. Enter the future date as the calculation date to see the projected age.
