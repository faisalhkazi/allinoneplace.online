Choosing an AI tool

AI tools are useful for different jobs: writing, coding, research, design, presentations, video editing and productivity. Instead of choosing a tool because it is popular, compare the task you need to complete, output quality, privacy options, pricing, usage limits and how easily the tool fits your workflow.

When using AI for important work, review the output yourself. AI-generated answers can contain errors, unsupported claims or outdated information.

How to evaluate an AI service

Start with a small real task and measure the result. Check whether the service stores prompts or uploaded files, whether business data is protected, and whether commercial-use rights match your needs.

Frequently asked questions

Is one AI tool best for everyone? No. Different tools have different strengths, pricing and privacy models.

Should AI output be trusted without review? No. Verify important facts and review generated code, documents or recommendations before using them.
