What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax used in India. This calculator helps estimate GST on a taxable amount and can show the total amount after tax. Depending on the transaction, GST may be represented through components such as CGST and SGST or IGST.

For an intra-state example with an 18% total GST rate, the rate is commonly split into 9% CGST and 9% SGST. The correct treatment depends on the nature and place of supply, so businesses should verify the applicable rules.

GST inclusive vs exclusive amounts

A GST-exclusive amount is the taxable base before GST. A GST-inclusive amount already contains GST. When issuing invoices or checking prices, confirm which type of amount you are working with before calculating.
