Since 2019

Six people, one warehouse, a small changing range.

Basket & Bloom started as a market stall selling ceramics one weekend a month. It's grown into a small studio, but the range stays deliberately small — we'd rather make forty things well than four hundred things adequately.

Made in-house

Ceramics, candles and textiles are all made or finished in our own workshop.

A range that changes

We retire products on purpose to keep the range small — nothing sits in the catalogue forever.

Packed by hand

Every order is packed by the same six people who make the products.
